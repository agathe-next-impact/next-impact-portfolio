# Web & mobile applications

This category gathers guides and resources on **custom applications** — when WordPress is no longer the right tool for your project.

## When to step outside the CMS

Three families of projects fall outside the WordPress scope:

- **Custom web app** — marketplace, business platform, internal tool, simulator. Dedicated business logic, user accounts, real-time data.
- **Mobile application (PWA)** — field tool, treasure hunt, app installable on smartphones without going through app stores.
- **Applicative extension** — a Headless site that needs to host an applicative zone (member area, configurator, e-learning).

## The autonomy promise

Just like with WordPress, you stay in control day to day. Every application I deliver comes with an **autonomous admin interface**, built for your business logic: you manage your content, your data and your users without ongoing technical dependency.
